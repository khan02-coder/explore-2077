DAILY LIFE // 2077

Life looks different from here.

HOME

Living above the clouds

Morning arrives from below. Sunrise climbs the glass walls of your residence while the city hums under a floor of cloud. Weather is something you watch, not something you walk through.

WORK

Working in virtual environments

The office is wherever the light is good. Teams meet in shared rooms made only of light, sketch with their hands in the air, and go home without ever having commuted.
